Derivatives — Participant-wise Open Interest

Latest reported outstanding long and short contracts, shown separately for index and stock futures.

—
Historical participant-wise OI snapshots are not yet present in the repository. Therefore daily/weekly change in contracts and percentage cannot yet be calculated reliably. Current long, short and net positions are displayed without estimating changes.

Net OI position = Long OI − Short OI. A positive net position indicates more long than short contracts in that segment; it is not, by itself, a standalone trading signal. Client and Pro are displayed for context only and do not influence Aurora stock selection or classification.

Source: institutional_data/derivatives_oi.csv • NSE participant-wise OI report
